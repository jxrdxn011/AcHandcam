**AcHandCam 0.1.0 — first public release.** Your phone becomes a handheld camera inside Assetto Corsa: walk, crouch, pan and tilt, and the game camera does the same, live, with the game shown on the phone like a camera viewfinder.

**How to install:** follow the [install guide](https://github.com/jxrdxn011/AcHandcam#install-about-20-minutes-the-first-time). Download the PC and AC files, plus the one for your phone:

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `AcHandCam-PC-0.1.0.zip` | PC receiver (needs Python 3.10+) |
| `AcHandCam-AC-app-0.1.0.zip` | Assetto Corsa app (drag onto Content Manager) |
| `AcHandCam-iPhone-0.1.0.zip` | iPhone app, iOS 15+ (install with Sideloadly) |
| `AcHandCam-Android-0.1.0.apk` | Android app, Android 7.0+ with ARCore — **experimental** |

**Features:** 6DOF handheld camera with freeze, recenter and pause · live game view on the phone · .5 / 1 / 2 / 3 lenses and pinch zoom · walk mode with a joystick · movement scale for small rooms · follow-car mode that stays locked to a moving car.

**Android is brand new and hasn't been tested on many phones yet.** If it misbehaves, please [open an issue](https://github.com/jxrdxn011/AcHandcam/issues) with your phone model and what you see.
